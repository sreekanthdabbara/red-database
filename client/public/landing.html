What is REED

One database. Every epidemiology answer.

REED consolidates published and public-domain epidemiology evidence into a curated, traceable, and regularly refreshed database. Each estimate links directly to its original source—so you can review context, methodology, and definitions with one click, and make decisions you can defend.

How REED is built

Man + Machine collaboration

Every record in REED is the product of systematic machine retrieval validated by domain experts—combining the scale of AI with the judgment of epidemiology researchers.

⚙ Machine 👤 Human
01

Automated literature retrieval Machine

Systematic crawls across PubMed, Embase, government health portals, and registry databases surface every relevant publication.

02

AI-assisted data extraction Machine

Structured extraction pulls epidemiological estimates, study populations, geographies, and endpoints from each source.

03

Expert quality curation Human

Epidemiology specialists validate entries, resolve conflicts, and flag methodological nuances no algorithm would catch.

04

Traceable, refreshed database Machine Human

Every estimate is linked to its source with continuous refresh cycles to keep REED current as evidence evolves.

What makes REED different

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Global coverage in one place

Epidemiology data from 50+ countries across all continents, consolidated so you stop stitching together data from a dozen sources.

Source-linked estimates

Every number traces back to its primary publication. Review definitions, methodology, and study population in two clicks—no detective work.

Regularly refreshed

Automated monitoring ensures new publications surface quickly. The data you access today reflects the current state of evidence, not last year's snapshot.

Curated, not just collected

Human epidemiologists review and annotate every record. Quality flags, methodological notes, and confidence grades come built in.

Disease-level depth

Coverage across 300+ rare diseases with endpoint-level granularity: incidence, prevalence, mortality, diagnosis lag, and patient journey milestones.

Cross-industry ready

Structured for life sciences, medtech, biotech, academic research, CROs, and payers—with outputs your team can use directly in submissions or strategy decks.
